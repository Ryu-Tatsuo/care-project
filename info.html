Customer care

Shipping, returns, contact and ingredients

Shipping

Orders are prepared in 2–4 business days. Complimentary delivery applies to orders of €70 or more; the care team confirms the available destination and final delivery estimate in WhatsApp.

Returns

Unopened, unused products may be requested for return within 30 days of delivery. Contact us before sending anything back so we can confirm eligibility and the return address.

Ingredients

Full ingredient lists and use guidance are available on every product detail panel. Botanical ingredients can still cause sensitivity; patch test first and discontinue use if irritation occurs. Consult a professional for medical or allergy advice.
