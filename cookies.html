Cookies

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Épure Botanique does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The shopping bag uses browser local storage so items remain available during a return visit on the same device.

Essential browser storage

The cart data contains product IDs, names, prices, currency, and quantities. It is not sent to the server by this consultation-order experience. Clearing browser storage removes the local bag.

Third parties

Fonts are currently requested from Google Fonts. Before production, choose whether to self-host fonts and document any remaining third-party services here.
